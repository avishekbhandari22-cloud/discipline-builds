# Privacy Policy - Discipline Builds

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Discipline Builds is a personal automation tool used by one person (the channel owner) to upload
original short films to their own YouTube channel.

**What the app accesses.** Only the permission to upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube
channel (the `youtube.upload` scope). It does not read your email, files, contacts or any other data.

**What is collected or stored.** The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any
visitor or user. The OAuth credentials are kept only as encrypted secrets in the owner's private
GitHub Actions settings and are used solely to upload the owner's own videos.

**Google user data.** The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
